# Domain Summary: Clinic AI Copilot

## Product Overview

Clinic AI Copilot is a simplified medical operations platform for clinic staff. It helps teams register patients, schedule appointments, capture medical notes, and use AI to summarize or search clinical information. The system is designed to support staff workflows, not replace clinical judgment.

The first product goal is a small, reliable workflow: a staff member registers a patient, creates an appointment, a doctor or nurse adds a medical note, the AI service generates a draft summary, and a human reviewer decides whether the AI output is accepted.

## Main Users

- Clinic admin: manages clinic operations, staff coordination, and appointment flow.
- Doctor: reviews patient context, writes medical notes, and validates AI-generated summaries.
- Nurse: supports patient intake, records observations, and assists with follow-up information.
- Patient support staff: handles registration, scheduling, reminders, and general patient communication.

## Sensitive Data To Protect

- Patient identity: name, date of birth, contact details, address, national ID, insurance ID.
- Medical information: symptoms, diagnoses, medications, allergies, lab results, imaging notes, medical history.
- Operational records: appointment details, staff actions, audit logs, internal comments.
- AI records: prompts, generated responses, retrieved context, embeddings metadata, review decisions.
- Authentication and infrastructure secrets: passwords, API keys, database URLs, tokens, private keys.

## AI Assistance Boundaries

AI is allowed to:

- Draft summaries from medical notes.
- Suggest structured highlights such as symptoms, medications, follow-up items, and missing context.
- Support semantic search over previous notes or approved clinic knowledge.
- Help staff find relevant historical information faster.

AI must not:

- Make final diagnoses.
- Prescribe treatment.
- Approve or modify medical records without human review.
- Hide uncertainty or present generated content as verified fact.
- Use real patient data in development, demos, logs, or training examples.

## Human Review Rule

Every AI output should be stored with a clear review status:

- Draft: generated by AI and not yet reviewed.
- Edited: modified by a human reviewer.
- Approved: accepted by an authorized staff member.
- Rejected: not suitable for use in the patient record.

The interface should make this state visible so staff understand whether content is AI-generated or clinically reviewed.

## Summary

Clinic AI Copilot is a staff dashboard for managing basic clinic workflows and responsibly adding AI support. The platform keeps structured clinic data, such as patients and appointments, in a reliable SQL database, while storing activity history, AI conversations, and unstructured notes in a NoSQL database. An AI service can summarize medical notes and help staff search previous context semantically, but all AI output remains draft content until a human reviews it.
